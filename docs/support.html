Reflect support

How can we help?

Reflect keeps your journal local-first. If something is not working as expected, the quickest route is an email with your platform, app version, and the step where the problem occurred. Please do not include private journal text.

Contact support

Email hello@fruhji.com

Include iOS or Android, your Reflect version, and a short description without sensitive journal content.

Purchases and restore

Open Reflect, go to Settings, then use Restore purchases. Billing, cancellation, and refunds are managed by the App Store or Google Play account used for the purchase.

Export, backup, and deletion

Settings lets you export your data, create an encrypted backup, or delete all in-app data. Files you previously saved through the system share sheet remain at the destination you chose and must be deleted there separately.
