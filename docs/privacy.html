Reflect

AI Journaling – Privacy Policy

Summary: Reflect is local-first. Your journal entries live on your device and are not uploaded to our servers — with one honest exception: when you tap Reflect on an entry, that entry's text (and the prompt) is sent to our AI proxy and on to Anthropic to generate a reflection. We do not keep your entries on the proxy beyond a short-lived, content-hashed cache used to avoid reprocessing the same request.

1. General

This privacy policy applies to the mobile app Reflect (referred to as "the App"). It describes what data the App processes, when, and why. No account is required to use the App.

2. What Stays on Your Device

Your journal entries, prompts, streaks, and settings are stored in a local database (SQLite) on your device. This data is not transmitted to us or anyone else, except for the explicit AI reflection request described in the next section.

3. AI Reflections

The AI reflection feature is opt-in per entry. It only runs when you actively tap the "Reflect" button. When you do:

  • The entry's text, the associated prompt, and your selected app language (so the reflection comes back in that language) are sent over an encrypted connection to our AI proxy.
  • The proxy forwards them to Anthropic (our AI provider) to generate the reflection, which is returned to your device.
  • Requests are briefly cached by a content hash (to avoid reprocessing identical requests) and rate-limited by IP address to prevent abuse. We do not build profiles from your entries and do not retain them for any other purpose.

If you never tap "Reflect", no entry content ever leaves your device.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by RevenueCat together with the Apple App Store and Google Play. We receive your subscription status (e.g. active / expired) so we can unlock paid features. We never receive or store your payment details.

5. Analytics

Analytics are optional and off unless a key is configured. When enabled, the App records anonymous product-usage events (for example: app opened, entry created, reflection used, paywall shown) via PostHog. The content of your journal entries is never included in analytics.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send your entries anywhere except the explicit AI reflection call you trigger.
  • We do not require an account or collect your identity.

7. Your Choices

  • Export your data at any time (Settings → Export data).
  • Delete all your data at any time (Settings → Delete all data) — this erases everything stored on the device.
  • Decline or revoke notification permission, and disable reminders, at any time.

8. Third Parties

Anthropic (AI processing of reflections you request) · RevenueCat (subscription management) · PostHog (anonymous analytics, only if enabled).

9. Children

The App is not directed at children. No account is created, and entry content stays on the device unless you explicitly request a reflection.

10. Changes to This Policy

If anything changes about how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the date below revised.
